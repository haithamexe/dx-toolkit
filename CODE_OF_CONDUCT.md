# Code of conduct

dx is made by volunteers, for developers. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, reviews or discussions, is expected to:

- **Be kind and assume good intent.** People come with different experience, languages and backgrounds.
- **Criticise code and ideas, never people.** Say what's wrong and, where you can, how to make it better.
- **Keep it on topic.** Issues and pull requests are for dx.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't post anyone's personal details, your own included, and remove them from logs and screenshots you share.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexual content, threats, and publishing someone's private information.

## If something goes wrong

Contact the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form, marking it as a conduct report, or ask in an issue for a private way to reach them without saying why. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who don't follow this code, in this repository and its other spaces.
